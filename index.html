MEET BURNACARE

A Research Concept with a Purpose

Burnacare is a student-led project exploring the concept of a microneedle patch incorporating marine collagen for potential applications in first-degree burn care.

Our goal is to understand the science, evaluate the proposed design, and explore its potential through appropriate research and testing.

THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE IDEA

Three Key Elements

Understanding the components behind the Burnacare research concept.

〰

Marine Collagen

Collagen is a structural protein studied in biomaterials and tissue engineering. Its suitability, stability, and performance depend on the formulation and supporting evidence.

⌁

Microneedle Technology

Microneedles are microscopic projections investigated for delivering substances through or into superficial skin layers. Their safety depends on their design and intended application.

✳

Wound Care Research

The project explores a proposed approach to first-degree burn care. Its effectiveness and suitability must be established through appropriate scientific evaluation.
